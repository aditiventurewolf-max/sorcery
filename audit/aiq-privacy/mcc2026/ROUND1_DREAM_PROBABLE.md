## Goa Medical College — FAIL

`gmcgoa.edu.in`, 130 URLs crawled, 64 documents opened, **10 roster hits**. The decisive files:

- `wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Final-Roll-No.of-MBBS-2022-23-1.pdf` — a Dean's office letter
  headed "... of 1st MBBS students admitted for the A.Y. 2022-23", columns
  **Name of the students / Gender**, serial-numbered rows.
- The same series for 2018-19 (90 rows), 2019-20 (99), 2021-22 (93).
- Year files `2018-19.pdf` (94 rows), `2019-20.pdf` (70), `2020-21.pdf` (33), `2021-2022.pdf` (93).
- Paramedical course lists (Occupational Therapy, Physiotherapy, Anaesthesia, Optometry).
- `/nmc` is a 480 KB disclosure hub.

This matters because Goa was otherwise the strongest new candidate: founded 1842, prestigious,
and the only college found so far besides Bhandara whose airport is inside the 1-2 hour rule
(Dabolim ~30 km, Mopa ~35 km from Panaji).

## Karnataka caveat

Mandya, Shimoga and Ballari all carry Karnataka's UG service bond: 1 year compulsory,
Rs 50 lakh penalty, and the **2024 amendment removed the buy-out option**, so it cannot be
paid off. Mandya is also ~130 km / ~3 hrs from Bengaluru airport, failing the flight rule.

## MCC 2026 reporting rules — changes the weight of distance

MCC introduced **one-time physical reporting** plus **online document verification** for 2026:

- **Freeze** (no upgrade wanted): report physically to the allotted institute once, for original
  document verification and fee payment.
- **Float** (willing to upgrade): upload scanned documents; the allotted college verifies them
  **online**, with no physical visit at that stage.

Upgradation runs to Round 3, then the Online Stray Vacancy Round. Colleges do **not** operate
nodal centres for AIQ UG verification — there is no proxy centre — but distance is now a
one-trip cost rather than a per-round cost. That argues for down-weighting connectivity
relative to privacy, which favours Akola and Latur.
